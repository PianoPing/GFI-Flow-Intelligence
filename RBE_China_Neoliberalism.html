RBE Vs. China RBE 与中国

Resource‑Based Economy · pragmatic resonance, fundamental divergence, and the paradigm gap that neoliberalism cannot grasp 资源基础型经济 · 务实共鸣、根本分野，以及新自由主义无法理解的范式鸿沟

Jacque Fresco's Resource‑Based Economy (RBE) envisions a socioeconomic system rooted in physical resources rather than monetary mechanisms. Though China has never adopted RBE as an official framework, its strategic planning—resource‑carrying capacity as a hard constraint, circularity as a pillar, and technology as a lever for efficiency—resonates deeply with the RBE ethos. Yet the two diverge fundamentally in institutional logic, governance scope, and driving forces.

雅克·弗雷斯科（Jacque Fresco）提出的"资源基础型经济"（Resource‑Based Economy, RBE）构想了一个以地球物理资源而非货币体系为核心决策依据的社会经济体系。中国虽未曾将RBE作为官方政策框架，但近年战略规划中展现的决策逻辑——以资源承载力为硬约束、以循环利用为支柱、以科技提升效率——与RBE精神产生了深刻共鸣。但二者在制度逻辑、治理范围和驱动力上存在根本分野。

Beyond this comparison, there is a deeper layer: why does the neoliberal mindset so often fail to comprehend China's resource‑oriented strategy? This essay argues that the answer lies not in policy details, but in two incommensurable rationalities—market rationality versus physical rationality.

在比较之外，还有一个更深层的追问：为什么新自由主义的思维方式，往往无法理解中国的资源导向战略？ 本文认为，答案不在于政策细节，而在于两种不可通约的理性范式——市场理性与物理理性。

I. Spiritual Affinity · Shared Decision Logic 一、精神契合 · 共享的决策逻辑

1.1 Carrying capacity as the hard constraint 1.1 以承载力为硬约束的空间治理

RBE insists that economic activity must respect the physical limits of natural systems. China's territorial spatial governance systematically embodies this logic. The Ministry of Natural Resources mandates spatial optimisation "balancing population, resources, environment, and economic‑ecological benefits," defining permanent basic farmland, ecological protection redlines, and urban development boundaries as "insurmountable red lines." By 2025, China had built 198 energy‑resource bases, with 14 minerals ranking first in global reserves and 17 in global production.

RBE强调经济活动必须服从自然系统的物理极限。中国的国土空间治理体系系统性地践行这一逻辑。自然资源部要求按照"人口资源环境相均衡、经济社会生态效益相统一"的原则优化空间格局，将永久基本农田、生态保护红线、城镇开发边界定义为"不可逾越的红线"。截至2025年，中国已建成198个能源资源基地，14种矿产储量居世界第一，17种矿产产量居全球首位。

1.2 Circular economy: from waste to "secondary resources" 1.2 循环经济：从废弃物到"第二资源"

One of RBE's most distinctive tenets: "there is no waste, only resources in the wrong place." China's 15th Five‑Year Plan for Circular Economy is a national‑scale implementation. By 2030, the major resource output rate will rise by 16%, comprehensive utilisation of bulk solid waste will reach 4.5 billion tons, and recycled resources will hit 510 million tons, with the industry value targeting ¥8 trillion. In 2025, scrap steel and non‑ferrous metals already supplied 27% and 30% of production feedstocks respectively.

RBE最具辨识度的理念之一是"没有废弃物，只有放错位置的资源"。《循环经济发展"十五五"规划》正是这一理念的国家级实践。到2030年，主要资源产出率提高16%，大宗固废年综合利用量达45亿吨，主要再生资源循环利用量达5.1亿吨，产业产值目标高达8万亿元。2025年，废钢铁、废有色金属已分别贡献了生产环节原料的27%和30%。

The plan prioritises recycling the "New Trio" — retired EV batteries, PV panels, and wind turbine blades — not merely for environmental reasons, but as a strategic resource security move: lithium, cobalt, and nickel are heavily import‑dependent. Building a domestic "secondary resource" base reduces vulnerability to global price volatility. The digital ID system for power batteries and the digital map for PV lifecycles signal a digitised, traceable circular economy.

规划特别强调补齐"新三样"——退役动力电池、光伏组件、风机叶片——的循环利用短板，这并非单纯环保考量，而是战略性资源保障：锂、钴、镍高度依赖进口，建立国内"第二资源"供应体系可降低对国际价格波动的脆弱性。动力电池"数字身份证"和光伏设备全生命周期"数字地图"的建立，标志着循环经济正向数字化、可追溯方向演进。

1.3 Strategic resource autonomy 1.3 战略性资源自主

China's critical mineral strategy mirrors the RBE emphasis on reducing reliance on global trade chains. As of 2025, China holds the world's largest reserves for 14 minerals including rare earths, tungsten, tin, molybdenum, antimony, gallium, germanium, indium, fluorite, and graphite. However, dependence on imports for copper, iron, lithium, cobalt, and nickel remains high — 11 minerals exceed 70% import reliance (copper 78.8%, nickel 84.9%, lithium 72.5%). The 15th FYP exploration push focuses on these shortfalls, aiming to raise recycled lithium to 30% by 2030, cutting overall dependence from 70% to 40%.

中国在关键矿产领域的战略布局与RBE强调的减少对全球贸易链依赖高度契合。截至2025年，中国已掌握稀土、钨、锡、钼、锑、镓、锗、铟、萤石、石墨等14种矿产储量的世界第一。但铜、铁、锂、钴、镍等紧缺矿产对外依存度仍然较高——11种矿产对外依存度超过70%（铜78.8%、镍84.9%、锂72.5%）。"十五五"找矿突破战略聚焦紧缺矿产，力争到2030年使回收锂占比达30%，将对外依存度从70%降至40%。

1.4 Technology as the substitute for scarcity 1.4 科技赋能：以技术进步替代稀缺

RBE posits technology as the primary solution to resource scarcity. China's "new quality productive forces" — innovation‑led, high‑tech, high‑efficiency, high‑quality — embody this thinking. The 15th FYP circular economy plan explicitly calls for "accelerating technological breakthroughs, developing major equipment, and building innovation platforms," and for the first time mandates deep AI application in sorting and recycling. This technology‑driven resource efficiency path aligns with the RBE technological orientation.

RBE认为科技是解决资源稀缺的核心路径。中国正在发展的"新质生产力"——以创新为主导，具有高科技、高效能、高质量特征——正是这一思维的体现。《循环经济发展"十五五"规划》明确提出"加快技术工艺和装备提升，开展重大技术装备攻关，建设资源循环利用技术创新平台"，并首次提出加强人工智能在回收分选领域的深度应用。这种以技术驱动资源效率的路径与RBE的技术主义取向一致。

II. Fundamental Divergence · Path and Logic 二、本质分野 · 路径与逻辑的差异

2.1 Institutional framework: market vs. technocratic governance 2.1 制度框架：市场机制 vs. 技术官僚治理

RBE envisions scientists and engineers directly calculating optimal resource allocation, abolishing money and markets. China operates within a socialist market economy: the state guides market actors through policy levers — subsidies, tax incentives, carbon quotas, mandatory standards — toward resource‑oriented goals. Money and markets remain the primary media for resource flows.

RBE设想由科学家和工程师直接计算资源最优配置，废除货币与市场。中国则是在社会主义市场经济框架内运作：政府通过政策杠杆——补贴、税收优惠、碳配额、强制性标准——引导市场力量响应资源导向目标。货币和市场依然是资源流动的主要媒介。

2.2 Governance scope: national strategy vs. global system 2.2 治理范围：国家战略 vs. 全球体系

RBE's ultimate aim is a border‑transcending global social reconfiguration. China's resource strategy operates within sovereign borders, enhancing national resource security and industrial competitiveness via technology and infrastructure. Though BRI resource cooperation exists, it remains a pragmatic state modernisation strategy, not an alternative global governance system.

RBE的终极目标是超越国界的全球社会重构。中国的资源战略则在主权国家边界内，通过科技和基建提升国家资源安全与产业竞争力。虽有"一带一路"框架下的资源合作，但其本质仍是一个主权国家务实现代化战略，而非全球治理体系的替代方案。

2.3 Driving force: top‑down planning vs. scientific consensus 2.3 驱动力：顶层规划 vs. 科学共识

RBE relies on global scientific consensus for coordinated governance. China's resource strategy is anchored in the legally binding Five‑Year Plans, designed at the central level and implemented via hierarchical decomposition. Terms like "capacity early warning and regulation" and "preventing disorderly entry of non‑core SOEs" reflect clear state‑led characteristics.

RBE依赖全球科学共识驱动的协同治理。中国的资源战略则依托具有法律效力的"五年规划"，由中央顶层设计、层层分解落实。"产能预警和有效调控""防范中央及地方非主责主业国有企业无序进入资源循环行业"等表述，清晰体现了国家主导特征。

2.4 Theoretical roots: indigenous innovation vs. Western framework 2.4 理论溯源：本土创新 vs. 西方框架

Interestingly, academic meta‑analyses (452 matched samples) reveal that classical Western resource‑based theories (RBV, resource dependence) have limited explanatory power for Chinese firms. Compared with U.S. firms, Chinese enterprises establish fewer business ties under resource dependence and transaction costs, yet extract more profit from managing generic strategic resources. This indicates a distinct indigenous resource‑management logic, unconnected to RBE theory but practically forming its own "resource‑first" approach.

有意思的是，学术元分析（452组匹配样本）表明，西方经典资源基础理论（RBV、资源依赖）在解释中国企业实践时存在局限。与美国企业相比，中国企业在面临资源依赖和交易成本时建立更少的商业关系，但在管理通用战略资源时能提取更多利润。这表明中国企业形成了独特的本土资源管理逻辑，与RBE理论并无直接关联，却在实践上形成了自己的"资源优先"路径。

III. Why Neoliberalism Cannot Grasp This Logic 三、为什么新自由主义无法理解这套逻辑

The neoliberal framework rests on several unchallenged premises:

新自由主义的思维框架建立在几个不可动摇的预设之上：

  • Markets are the only legitimate resource allocator — price signals automatically guide optimal distribution.
  • Government intervention inevitably distorts and reduces efficiency — "the less governance, the better."
  • Individual rationality aggregates into collective optimality — the invisible hand self‑corrects.
  • Short‑term profit maximisation is the sole corporate objective — shareholder value supremacy.
  • 市场是唯一的资源配置机制——价格信号自动引导最优分配。
  • 政府干预必然导致扭曲和低效——"管得越少越好"。
  • 个体理性汇聚即为集体最优——看不见的手自动调节。
  • 短期利润最大化是企业唯一目标——股东价值至上。

China's resource‑oriented strategic planning diverges from all four of these assumptions:

而中国"资源导向型"的战略规划，在这四个维度上都走了一条截然不同的路：

3.1 Two incommensurable rationalities 3.1 两种不可通约的理性

Neoliberal "rationality" is market rationality — price signals, marginal returns, capital ROI.

新自由主义的"理性"是市场理性——价格信号、边际收益、资本回报率。

China's resource‑oriented rationality is physical rationality — resource carrying capacity, energy conversion efficiency, material circularity, supply‑chain physical security.

中国资源导向战略的"理性"是物理理性——资源承载力、能量转换效率、物质循环闭合度、供应链物理安全。

The former cannot comprehend the latter, because their accounting methods are fundamentally different.

前者无法理解后者，因为两者的"算账方式"完全不同。

  • Market rationality asks: "Does this make money?"
  • Physical rationality asks: "Does this do more with less physical resources? Does this make the nation more resource‑secure?"
  • 市场理性问："这能赚钱吗？"
  • 物理理性问："这能用更少的资源做更多的事吗？这能让国家在资源上更安全吗？"

When China's 15th FYP mandates that bulk solid waste utilisation reach 4.5 billion tons and major resource output rate rise by 16%, it is not calculating financial returns — it is calculating material balance. This is a mode of thinking rooted in ecological economics and engineering, not neoclassical economics.

当中国的"十五五"规划要求大宗固废年利用量达到45亿吨、主要资源产出率提高16%时，它不是在算经济账——它是在算物质平衡账。这是一种生态经济学和工程学的思维，而非新古典经济学的思维。

3.2 State capacity: the neglected variable 3.2 国家能力：被忽视的变量

Neoliberal theory treats the state as a "necessary evil"—as small as possible, as non‑interventionist as possible.

新自由主义理论中，国家是一个"必要的恶"——尽可能小，尽可能少干预。

But China's resource‑oriented strategy presupposes that the state must possess sufficient institutional, data, and enforcement capacity to implement long‑term resource planning.

但中国资源导向战略的前提是：国家必须拥有足够的制度能力、数据能力、执行能力来实施长期资源规划。

  • Unified "three‑rate" standards (mining recovery, processing recovery, comprehensive utilisation) across all 125 mineral types
  • Over 5,500 provincial‑level or above green mines established
  • National "One‑Map" big‑data platform for natural resources
  • Five‑Year Plans decomposing national targets down to provincial, municipal, and county levels
  • 覆盖全部125个矿种的"三率"标准（开采回采率、选矿回收率、综合利用率）
  • 建成5500余家省级以上绿色矿山
  • 全国统一的自然资源"一张图"大数据平台
  • 通过"五年规划"将国家目标层层分解到省、市、县

All of this requires strong national data collection, administrative execution, and policy coordination capacity — capacities that neoliberalism presumes states do not or should not possess.

这一切需要强大的国家数据采集能力、行政执行能力和政策协调能力——而新自由主义恰恰预设了国家不具备或不应当具备这种能力。

3.3 The limits of perspective 3.3 视角的局限性

Neoliberalism fails to comprehend China not because it is unintelligent, but because:

新自由主义之所以"看不懂"中国，并非因为它不够聪明，而是因为：

  1. Its analytical tools are designed for market transactions, not for physical resource management.
  2. Its value premises reject state‑led long‑term planning as inherently "inefficient" and "authoritarian."
  3. Its time horizon is quarterly earnings and electoral cycles, while China's resource strategy operates on "15th FYP" (2030), "2035 Vision", and "2060 Carbon Neutrality" timelines.
  1. 它的分析工具是为"市场交易"设计的，而非为"物理资源管理"设计的。
  2. 它的价值预设排斥国家主导的长期规划，将其天然视为"低效"和"威权"。
  3. 它的时间尺度是季度财报和选举周期，而中国资源战略的时间尺度是"十五五"（2030）、"2035远景目标"、"2060碳中和"。

When your ruler only measures markets, you cannot see physical resource realities. When your horizon is only four years, you cannot see resource security thirty years ahead.

当你的尺子只能量市场，你就看不见资源物理现实；当你的视野只有四年，你就看不见三十年后的资源安全。

IV. Conclusion · Spirit Aligned, Paths Apart, Paradigms Incommensurable 四、结论 · 精神契合，路径分立，范式不可通约

If Fresco's RBE represents the ultimate ideal of resource‑orientation, China's current practice is the most pragmatic embedding of "resource physics" into the core of decision‑making within the existing global political‑economic framework.

如果说弗雷斯科的RBE是"资源导向"的终极理想形态，那么中国目前的实践，则是在现有全球政治经济框架内，最大程度将"资源物理逻辑"嵌入决策核心的务实尝试。

The resonance between China and RBE stems from a shared premise: economic decisions must be bounded by physical resource realities, boosted by technology, and sustained through circularity. But the divergence is equally profound: China does not abolish the market but steers it; it does not build a global government but focuses on national resource security; it does not rely on scientific‑consensus‑driven global coordination but on legally‑binding national planning.

中国与RBE的共鸣，源于共同的认知前提：经济决策须以资源物理现实为底线、以科技提升效率、以循环构建可持续供给。但两者的分野同样深刻：中国未废除市场，而是引导市场；未建立全球政府，而是聚焦国家资源安全；不依赖科学共识主导的全球协同，而依托具有法律效力的国家规划。

China's development model is not a direct response to RBE theory, but the resonance in the fundamental spirit — "disciplining economic decisions by resource realities" — makes it an important reference for understanding China's strategic logic. If RBE is a visionary blueprint, China is translating its most pragmatic components — resource efficiency, circularity, technological substitution — into enforceable national policy, under real‑world constraints.

中国的发展模式并非对RBE理论的直接响应，但二者在"以资源现实规约经济决策"这一根本精神上的共鸣，使其成为理解中国战略逻辑的一个重要参照系。 如果说RBE是一个关于资源社会的终极构想，那么中国正在做的，是在现实约束下，将这一构想中最务实的部分——资源效率、循环利用、技术替代——转化为可执行的国策。

At a deeper level, what neoliberalism cannot comprehend is not a policy choice, but an entirely different rationality — one that measures success not by quarterly returns but by physical balance, resource security, and long‑term national resilience. This is not a failure of intelligence, but a failure of paradigm.

在更深层次上，新自由主义无法理解的不是某项政策选择，而是一种完全不同的理性范式——它衡量成功的标准不是季度回报，而是物质平衡、资源安全和长期国家韧性。这不是智识的失败，而是范式的失败。
